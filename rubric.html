ABER and Oral History: Comparison Rubric

Preliminary Rubric (Automated Biographical Elicitation and Re-tell)

Stephen Mucher, Ph.D. · Cornell Tech · Fall 2026

This is the preliminary version of the rubric. It consolidates the disciplinary practices of oral history in a form that facilitates comparison with auto-autobiography. It holds 33 criteria in four categories. Each criterion has a short descriptor and a prompt that can be put to any agent, human or machine.

Conventions

Agent
Always singular, with no assumption about kind. The agent may be human or machine.
Narrator
The person interviewed, in the OHA's term.
Stage nouns
Elicit prompts refer to the inquiry, Re-tell prompts to the account, Keeping prompts to the record.
Reading
The rubric is mainly descriptive: each prompt asks how. The Setup tab also gives a binary reading that asks whether.
Stages
Each criterion is described once. Significance and Contextualization are described at both the Elicit and Re-tell stages.
Unmet criteria
Where an agent cannot address a criterion at all, the gap is itself a finding.
Links
Each anchor links to its statement page. The OHA pages have no link targets for individual numbered items, so OHA links open the statement or its section.
