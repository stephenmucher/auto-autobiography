# Auto-autobiography and Oral History

A rubric comparing oral history practice with Automated Biographical Elicitation and Re-tell (ABER).

Stephen Mucher, Ph.D. · 2026 Visiting Fellow, Digital Life Initiative, Cornell Tech · [ORCID 0009-0000-8310-0469](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-8310-0469)

**Read the site at <https://auto-autobiography.stephenmucher.org>**

## What this is

Living memoir platforms such as Storyworth, Remento, and Autobiographer use algorithmic question protocols or conversational AI to elicit an older adult's reminiscences, reformulate them, and return them as an audience-sensitive narrative in book form. I call this two-stage process Automated Biographical Elicitation and Re-tell (ABER). I call its product auto-autobiography, a life narrative grounded in the documented reminiscences of a human author but retold without clear methodological transparency.

This repository holds a preliminary rubric that consolidates the disciplinary practices of oral history in a form that facilitates comparison with auto-autobiography. It draws on the Principles and Best Practices of the Oral History Association (2018), the AHA History Discipline Core (2016), and the NCHS Historical Thinking Standards (1996).

## The rubric

33 criteria in four categories.

| Category | Criteria | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Elicit | 12 | How the inquiry is prepared and conducted |
| Re-tell | 7 | How the inquiry results are described |
| Interpret | 9 | What historical thinking practices are applied in elicitation and re-tell |
| Retain | 5 | How the record is kept, described, accessed, and owned |

## Feedback

Criticism is the purpose of publishing a preliminary version. [Open an issue](https://github.com/stephenmucher/auto-autobiography/issues/new/choose) and choose the form that fits.

- Historians and oral historians. Which criteria are wrong, missing, or miscredited?
- Professionals who design elicitation systems. How would your agent address one of these prompts?
- Consumers familiar with ABER products through a parent or grandparent. What did it ask, and what did it miss?
